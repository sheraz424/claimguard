Purpose of the System

Lost items on campus—laptops, calculators, ID cards, power banks—are often returned through informal, unverified handovers. Anyone can claim an item by simply describing it, leading to fraudulent claims and disputes.

ClaimGuard solves this by separating public descriptions from private ownership evidence. Claims succeed only when the claimant proves knowledge of protected evidence, verified by a Security Officer.

Who Benefits?
  • Students — secure recovery of belongings
  • Security Staff — verified handovers with audit trail
  • University Admin — reduced disputes and accountability

How It Works

Describe your item publicly. Store private evidence (serial, mark, receipt) securely—never shown publicly.

Submit Claim

Prove ownership by providing knowledge of private evidence. Limited attempts prevent brute-force.

Verified Handover

Security Officer reviews, approves, and records the handover. Full audit log for accountability.
